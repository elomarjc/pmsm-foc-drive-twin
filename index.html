⚡ PMSM Field-Oriented Control (FOC) Drive Twin

🎯 The Industrial Problem Solved

Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors (PMSMs) deliver high power density in industrial manufacturing, electric vehicles, and pumps. Achieving high efficiency requires decoupled control of magnetic flux (id = 0) and torque (iq). However, optical shaft encoders commonly fail in harsh factory environments (vibration, heat, dust). Variable frequency drive engineers need sensorless control algorithms to reconstruct rotor position from electrical measurements.

💡 How This Digital Twin Solves It

Models 3-phase PMSM electro-mechanical dynamics, Clarke and Park vector transformations, Space Vector PWM (SVPWM) with 7-segment sector duty-cycle switching, inverter dead-time harmonic compensation, and a sensorless Sliding Mode Observer (SMO) that reconstructs back-EMF.

🕹️ Interactive Quickstart Tutorial (Try This Now!)

  • 1
    Speed Regulation & Decoupled Torque: Adjust Target Speed (e.g. 1,500 RPM) and observe how the Vector Scope pins direct-axis flux to id = 0 A while torque current iq responds to load.
  • 2

  • 3
    Sensorless SMO Feedback: Toggle Sensorless Feedback Mode (SMO) ON: the drive disconnects the physical encoder and tracks rotor angle purely via the sliding mode back-EMF observer with <2.5° error.

📊 Telemetry & Control Glossary

  • Clarke & Park Transforms: Vector transformations that convert 3-phase stationary stator currents (ia, ib, ic) into a 2-phase rotating reference frame (id, iq) synchronized with the rotor magnetic field.
  • SVPWM (Space Vector PWM): Digital switching technique that generates inverter gating pulses from 8 fundamental voltage vectors, delivering 15.5% higher DC-bus voltage utilization than sinusoidal PWM.
  • Sliding Mode Observer (SMO): Non-linear state observer that forces current estimation errors onto a sliding manifold to reconstruct motor back-EMF and rotor angle without shaft sensors.
